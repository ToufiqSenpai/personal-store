# SR12 SEO landscape and verifiable facts (for `/sr12`)

Resolves: https://github.com/ToufiqSenpai/personal-store/issues/4

Research date: 2026-11-24. All dates below are publication dates as printed on the source
(or, for undated live pages, the access date is stated explicitly).

## Method and limitations

- The repo's `websearch` integration is broken (returns "No search results found" for all
  queries), so discovery used Google News RSS (`news.google.com/rss/search?q=...&hl=id&gl=ID&ceid=ID:id`),
  with Google News article IDs decoded to original publisher URLs, then each article fetched
  directly and read.
- **No live web SERP data was obtainable.** Google web search returns a JS redirect interstitial,
  Bing returns irrelevant results (Google Translate pages), and DuckDuckGo/Brave are blocked or
  rate-limited. Therefore **no search volume, keyword difficulty, CPC or ranking data is available**.
  All keyword recommendations below are evidence-based hypotheses derived from media vocabulary
  and observable competitor content, not measured metrics.
- BPOM numbers and halal certificate IDs were **not** available to this research and are **not**
  stated anywhere in this document. Media claims that products are "terdaftar BPOM" are reproduced
  only as claims, with the caveat that registration is not efficacy approval.
- Much SR12 coverage is syndicated press release / advertiser content. This is flagged per source;
  it affects how much trust a "featured in" strip can fairly claim.

## 3. Is an official SR12 site or marketplace indexed?

**Finding: no official corporate website or official marketplace store could be verified.** What
exists publicly are third-party distributor/partner sites and social accounts.

- Media refer to an official website without naming it: Inforadar.id, 2024-01-23
  (https://inforadar.disway.id/news/read/663692/gaet-ustad-solmed-jadi-ba-sr12-ini-fakta-menarik-tentang-perusahaan-herbal-dan-skincare-ini) says facts were "Dikutip dari website Web Official SR 12" and captions an image "Website Official SR12" — but provides no URL.
- `https://sr12.id/` (accessed 2026-11-24) is a **distributor recruitment site**, self-described
  "Distributor Resmi SR12 — Mitra resmi SR12 Herbal Skincare", with partner biolinks at
  `sr12.id/<name>`, a partner signup form, and "Harga sewaktu-waktu dapat berubah mengikuti
  kebijakan PT SR12 Herbal Kosmetik". It is not corporate-owned as far as the page discloses.
  Notably it labels its own figures "*Angka publikasi 2022" — useful corroboration that the
  "80.000+ mitra" number is a 2022 publication figure, not a current one.
- `https://www.sr12herbal.com/` (accessed 2026-11-24) is **"RAHASIA AYU SR12"**, an individual
  reseller webstore (Simpeltoko platform) with a Bogor address and WhatsApp number; footer
  "Distributor resmi sr12". Not an official corporate site.
- Instagram handles cited in press conflict: `@sr12official_id` (Suara.com, 2022-10-23; Warta
  Ekonomi, 2022-10-21) vs `@sr12herbalskincareofficial_` (Media Indonesia, 2021-12-07). Which is
  current is **unverified**.
- Shopee/Tokopedia search pages load results via JavaScript; no "official store" marker was
  extractable from the served HTML (checked 2026-11-24). **Unverified**, not confirmed absent.
- Indonesian Wikipedia has no SR12 article (404 on https://id.wikipedia.org/wiki/SR12, accessed
  2026-11-24) — a trust/entity signal is missing, and also an opening for an explainer page.
- Caveat: because general web SERP access was unavailable (see Method), "indexed" in the strict
  Google-web-index sense could not be tested. What **is** verifiable is that distributor sites and
  extensive news coverage are publicly reachable and surfaced by Google News.

**SEO reading:** there is no dominant official first-party page owning brand queries. The pages
competing for brand intent are media articles plus a distributor site that already publishes
10+ SEO articles (see section 5). That is a winnable but non-empty gap.

## 5. Realistic keyword targets for a brand-new, low-authority domain

Strategy assumed (already decided at charting, not re-decided here): category intent for
discovery + brand intent for capture; SEO/conversion balanced. Because no SERP metrics exist,
these are **hypotheses grounded in the observed vocabulary and content gap**, not measured keywords.

**Do not target as primary keywords:** "skincare", "skincare herbal", "produk kecantikan",
"susu kambing etawa" alone, "cara menghilangkan flek hitam" — these are broad head terms where
publication volume from large outlets and established beauty/health sites is high (examples:
Beautynesia 2020-11-14, Fimela 2023-03-09, Hello Sehat 2023-05-02, Halodoc 2026-06-30 as surfaced
in Google News RSS). A new domain cannot compete there; use them only as secondary on-page terms.

**Competitor content check:** `sr12.id` (accessed 2026-11-24) already publishes articles titled
"Manfaat GoMilku Gold SR12…", "Keuntungan Menjadi Mitra SR12…", "Daftar Harga SR12 Terbaru…",
"SR12 Itu MLM atau Bukan?…", "Skincare Aman untuk Ibu Hamil dan Menyusui…", "Cara Menghilangkan
Flek Hitam di Wajah…", "Susu Kambing Etawa: Manfaat…", "30 Kata-kata Promosi Jualan Skincare…".
The obvious brand+commercial long-tails are therefore already contested by a partner site with a
large dynamic URL footprint. Differentiation must come from sourcing, neutrality and dating.

| Target query (hypothesis) | Intent | Evidence that demand/space exists | Why achievable |
|---|---|---|---|
| "SR12 itu apa" / "SR12 herbal skincare penjelasan" | Brand discovery | No official corporate explainer page surfaced in coverage; Inforadar 2024-01-23 refers to an official site without a URL; no Wikipedia entry (404, 2026-11-24) | Thin, fragmented exact-intent results; a sourced explainer can own it |
| "sejarah SR12" / "SR12 didirikan tahun berapa" / "Toni Firmansyah SR12" | Brand/entity | Founding year and anniversary conflict across ANTARA 2025-01-24, Kontan 2019-08-04, Suara.com 2024-01-21, Langgam.id 2026-02-10 | A single dated, source-cited answer beats duplicated press releases |
| "jumlah mitra SR12" / "SR12 80 ribu mitra" | Brand/trust | Headline/body contradiction in SWA 2022-10-21; later lower/conflicting counts (Langgam.id 2024-06-18; JPNN 2024-02-23) | Directly resolves a confusing, frequently copied statistic |
| "Massage Oil Bulus SR12" / "minyak bulus SR12 manfaat" | Product discovery | Orami 2021-09-01; Riau Online 2019-04-22 | Exact named product; low number of dedicated pages; avoid efficacy claims |
| "Gomilku SR12" / "susu kambing etawa SR12" | Product discovery | Media Indonesia 2021-12-07; sr12.id article (accessed 2026-11-24) | Named SKU, modest dedicated coverage |
| "SR12 BPOM" / "produk SR12 halal" | Trust | Claims appear in SWA 2022-10-21 ("100% bahan alami"), Orami 2021-09-01 ("terdaftar BPOM"), sr12.id (accessed 2026-11-24); no authoritative answer page found | High trust intent, weak authoritative supply — **but do not invent numbers**; link to official BPOM/halal lookup when the knowledge sheet arrives |
| "PT SR12 Herbal Kosmetik" / "SR12 CMM Group" / "PT CMM Global Internasional" | Entity/trust | Entity name changes across Kontan 2019-08-04, ANTARA 2025-01-24, Langgam.id 2026-02-10, Padangkita.com 2026-02-11 | Very low competition, high value for due-diligence queries |
| "SR12 Padang training center" / "SR12 Pesisir Selatan" / "SR12 Pangkalpinang" | Local discovery | Langgam.id 2026-04-11; Langgam.id 2026-02-10; Fakta Berita 2025-11-08 | Geo-qualified long-tails with a handful of fresh news pages; regional angle is a genuine differentiator |
| "cara jadi mitra SR12" / "SR12 MLM atau bukan" | Commercial | Kontan 2019-08-04 (Toni's own MLM sales history); sr12.id article (accessed 2026-11-24) | Contested by sr12.id; only pursue with a genuinely more transparent, sourced treatment |

**Recommended page shape implied by the evidence:** one canonical, dated `/sr12` explainer that
(a) states the category in media's own words ("skincare herbal / herbal dan skincare"), (b) answers
the identity/entity/founding questions with inline source links and dates, (c) explicitly resolves
the 80 mitra / 80 ribu mitra confusion, (d) lists only product names that sources actually mention,
and (e) carries a short trust strip of Tier A outlets from section 2. Avoid unverifiable BPOM/halal
IDs, health-benefit claims, and present-tense scale numbers.
